Your home runs on reminders. Most of them live in your head.

Lintel moves them out of group chats, fridge notes and memory, into one shared plan that tells the right person at the right time.

The usual way

  • The generator service is overdue, and nobody noticed until it stopped.
  • Chores are argued over in the family group chat, again.
  • Warranty cards are somewhere in a kitchen drawer, probably.
  • Five apps for five devices, and none of them talk to each other.

With Lintel

  • Service dates are scheduled once and repeat on their own.
  • Chores rotate fairly, and everyone sees whose turn it is.
  • Every receipt and warranty is a photo away, with expiry alerts.
  • Lights, cameras and sensors live in the same place as the plan.
