Logging

Everything the house pays for, with who paid.

Add an expense in seconds: amount, date, what it was for, the category and who paid. Attach the receipt or invoice, link the provider, and add a note.

  • Ready-made categories. Electricity, fuel and the generator, water, internet, repairs, cleaning, security, estate dues, rent, groceries, cooking gas and more, with your own sub-categories.
  • Search and filter. By month, category, provider, who paid or whether there’s a receipt. Export what you see to a spreadsheet.
  • Nothing lost by mistake. Deleted expenses wait 30 days in Recently deleted before they’re gone.

Receipts

Snap the receipt. Lintel fills in the rest.

Take a photo or pick a PDF, and the amount, date and shop are read on your phone, with the category it was filed under last time. Nothing you’ve already typed is overwritten.

  • Read on your device. Receipts aren’t sent anywhere to be read. If you attach one, it’s stored privately with the expense.
  • A pile at once. Pick up to 20 receipts and review a draft expense for each, then save them together.
  • Duplicates caught. A receipt that matches an expense you’ve already logged is flagged before you save it twice.

Budgets and insights

See where the month went.

Give each category, or sub-category, a monthly budget. Insights show the month by category, your top providers and how each budget is holding.

  • Month by month. Step back through earlier months, or pick which month a bill counts towards.
  • Bills that come round again. Rent, dues and subscriptions turn into expenses on their own, waiting for a tap to confirm.
  • Income, for household admins. Record what comes in, including regular income, and see the month’s net. Hide the figures on screen when someone’s looking over your shoulder.
