Upkeep

Set the job once. Get reminded before it’s due.

Servicing the generator, fumigation, washing the water tank, paying the security levy: Lintel keeps the jobs that keep the house running, and the people who do them.

Tasks

The house’s recurring jobs, never forgotten.

Add a job once with how often it comes round, and Lintel emails a reminder in the morning, days before it’s due, and again if it’s overdue.

  • Any schedule. One-off, or daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly; on fixed dates, or counted from when it was last done.
  • The right person. Assign a task to someone and the reminder goes to them; otherwise everyone in the house hears about it.
  • Done, and what it cost. Mark it done and log the cost as an expense in the same step. Each task keeps its history.

Providers

The people you trust, a tap away.

Save your plumber, electrician, generator technician and gas vendor once, for the whole house, and call or WhatsApp them in one tap.

  • Everything about them. Trade, phone number, WhatsApp, email, area, a rating and your own notes.
  • Their history with you. What you’ve spent with each provider and the jobs they look after.
  • Linked to the work. Attach a provider to a task or an expense, so their number is right there when it’s due.
