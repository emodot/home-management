Questions

Good to know.

The things people ask before they move their household into Lintel.

The basics

Who is Lintel for?

Anyone who shares a home and its running: couples, families, flatmates, or someone who helps run a parent’s house. Everyone sees the same expenses, list, pantry, tasks and providers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open Lintel in your phone’s browser and sign in. To keep it on your home screen, use your browser’s “Add to Home Screen” or “Install app”.

How do I join?

With an invite link from your household admin. Lintel is invite-only for now: the Lintel team sets each household up for its admin, who then invites everyone else. How joining works

Can I be in more than one household?

Yes. Switch between them from the household name at the top of the app.

Money and receipts

Are my receipts sent somewhere to be read?

No. Receipts are read on your own device. If you attach the photo or PDF to an expense, it’s stored privately with your household’s data.

What currency does it use?

Naira by default.

Who can see our income?

Only household admins. Everyone can see the household’s expenses.

What if I delete something by mistake?

Deleted expenses, receipts, income, tasks and providers wait 30 days in Recently deleted, where you can bring them back.

Kitchen

Does it work without signal?

The shopping list does, once it’s open. In shopping mode you can keep checking things off and pricing them with no connection; the changes are saved on your phone and sent when you’re back online, even if you close the app first.

Finishing the trip, and the rest of Lintel, need a connection.

Which units does the kitchen understand?

Kilos, grams, litres and millilitres; pieces, packs, tins, sachets and bunches; and market measures: derica, mudu, paint buckets, bags, crates and tubers. You can teach an item how its units relate, like “1 bag = 50 kg”.

Privacy

Who can see our household?

Only its members. Each household is separate, and people join only through an invite from one of its admins.

Can a member leave?

Yes, from Members. A household’s last admin can’t leave while others remain, so the house is never left without one.
