A day with Lintel

Less chasing, fewer surprises.

The house keeps running without anyone holding it all in their head, or in the family group chat.

  1. 07:00

    A reminder before the generator needs it

    Bola’s inbox: “Service the generator is due in 3 days”, with Emeka’s number one tap away.

  2. 10:30

    The signal drops at Mile 12

    Chi keeps checking off the list and entering prices. Lintel holds the changes and sends them when the network is back.

  3. 11:15

    Shopping done, books done

    Finish the trip: it’s one expense, every price is saved for next time, and what was bought is in the pantry.

  4. 19:00

    Jollof for Sunday

    The recipe says the pantry is short of pepper. One tap puts it on the list, and everyone sees it straight away.
