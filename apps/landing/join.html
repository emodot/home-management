Three steps

From invite to a house that runs itself.

  1. Ask for early access

    Tell us a little about your household. When there’s room, the Lintel team sets it up and emails you an invite as its admin.

  2. Open the link

    Create your account, or sign in if you already have one, and you’re in. A short tour shows you around.

  3. Bring in the house

    Invite the people you live with from Members, by link or email. They see everything as soon as they join.

Already have an account?
